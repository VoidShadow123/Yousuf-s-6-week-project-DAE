Why You Should Buy a Car

5 reasons you should buy a car include, but are not limited to

    - Cars are faster than walking
    - Cars can get you anywhere you want, as long as you pay for gas
    - Cars are convinient
    - They can hold an average amount of 5 people
    - The average car can get you to 100 mph

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Potential Downsides

Buying a car is a big financial decision, as it may cause you to go in debt.

Another reason buying a car may be a downside, is because gas is very expensive

Finally, Cars are quite difficult to maintain, and you may be forced to see a mechanic,

if you don't have the knowledge or tools to repair your car. Mechanics are quite expensive
